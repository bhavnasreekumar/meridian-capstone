# Interview answer template

**Before recording:** Use an approved note-taking location. Do not record customer or employee data in this AI-accessible wiki. This template does not authorize unrestricted raw notes.

**Before AI access:** A student must check completed notes for accuracy and restricted information, remove prohibited contents, and confirm approval and the source location before the AI reads them. Student review cannot override Meridian's restrictions. Follow the [data-handling checklist](../../docs/data-handling-checklist.md).
