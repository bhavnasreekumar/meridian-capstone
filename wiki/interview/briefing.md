# Briefing for Dana's interview

## Meridian's stated situation

Meridian requests a dashboard comparing store and category sales to inform expansion. Dana favors Pasadena, while naming revenue, costs, and customer experience as goals without quantified success targets. The brief proposes preliminary board materials in three weeks and an approximately eight-week project; exact dates require confirmation. [Client brief](../../raw/client-brief.md); [business priorities](../topics/business-priorities.md).

Store growth has been uneven, and a POS migration occurred last spring. Store maturity and comparability are questions to investigate, not established explanations of performance. [Client brief](../../raw/client-brief.md); [store performance](../topics/store-performance.md).

These priorities derive from unresolved questions in [business priorities](../topics/business-priorities.md), [expansion criteria](../topics/expansion-criteria.md), and [data constraints](../topics/data-constraints.md); they are not decisions already made by Dana.
