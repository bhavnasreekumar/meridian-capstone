# Expansion criteria

## Meridian statements

Dana favors Pasadena as the next site and wants data to support the decision. Meridian describes its positioning around prepared foods, local sourcing, and a smaller footprint. It reports expanding partly through leases taken over from departing chains in neighborhoods it considered underserved. [Client brief](../../raw/client-brief.md)

## External findings

ICSC describes flexible layouts and Southern California micromarkets. [Original article](https://www.icsc.com/news-and-views/icsc-exchange/new-grocery-formats-store-growth-and-investment-signal-sector-in-motion); [S001 summary and limitations](../sources/s001.md).

## Team interpretations and hypotheses

SafeGraph's checklist suggests questions about trade areas and physical constraints, not predetermined criteria. [Source and limitations](../sources/s002.md).

Pasadena is a candidate to evaluate. The brief does not provide a comparison of alternative sites or evidence that Pasadena meets agreed criteria. Research should help frame those criteria and identify evidence that could support or challenge the preference. [Basis: client brief](../../raw/client-brief.md)

Potential criteria to discuss include local demand, competitive context, property costs, operating fit, and effects on existing stores. These are team hypotheses for discussion, not Meridian-approved criteria or findings about any location.
