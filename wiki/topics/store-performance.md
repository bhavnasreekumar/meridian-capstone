# Store performance

## Meridian statements

Meridian reports uneven growth across stores and requests comparisons by store and category. The brief identifies store size and opening dates as available attributes and reports a POS migration last spring. It does not define comparable-store performance or a preferred success metric. [Client brief](../../raw/client-brief.md)

## Team interpretations and hypotheses

JLL's positioning discussion motivates clarification of Meridian's proposition; visit growth must not be read as revenue growth. [Source and limitations](../sources/s003.md).

[SafeGraph's framework](../sources/s002.md) motivates checking forecasts against outcomes; it supplies no validated Meridian benchmark.

Candidate comparisons include growth over a comparable period and sales relative to store size. Store maturity, seasonality, and changes in data definitions could affect comparisons. These are analytical hypotheses to investigate, not validated explanations of Meridian's results. They arise from the growth, store attributes, and migration described in the [brief](../../raw/client-brief.md).

Sales alone may not settle questions about operating costs or customer experience; confirm which outcomes the dashboard must represent. See [business priorities](business-priorities.md).
