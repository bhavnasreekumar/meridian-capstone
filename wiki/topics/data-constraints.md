# Data constraints

## Meridian statements

The brief names POS transactions, loyalty records, labor schedules and hours, and store attributes. Marcus is the extract contact after NDA signature. No receipt or verification of these datasets has been established by the brief. [Client brief](../../raw/client-brief.md)

The brief prohibits customer and employee data, including excerpts, from entering AI tools. It expressly allows sales totals by store and week and store attributes. The team's [data-handling checklist](../../docs/data-handling-checklist.md) additionally limits students to aggregated summaries and defines approval, transfer, incident, sharing, and closeout procedures.

## Team interpretations and hypotheses

The requested category analysis and the explicitly permitted store-by-week sales data are not identical scopes. Category-level sales AI use needs written clarification; the team cannot infer permission merely because data is aggregated. This interpretation follows the [brief's permission boundary](../../raw/client-brief.md) and the [checklist](../../docs/data-handling-checklist.md).

Migration-related comparability requires investigation rather than an assumption that old and new extracts are consistent. See [store performance](store-performance.md).
