# Business priorities

## Meridian statements

Dana requests a sales dashboard by store and category to support a location decision. She also names revenue growth, lower operating costs, and improved customer experience as success goals. The brief proposes an approximately eight-week project and preliminary board materials in three weeks. [Client brief](../../raw/client-brief.md)

## Team interpretations and hypotheses

The dashboard is a proposed deliverable; the decision it must enable needs clarification. The broad goals may require prioritization if their measures or recommendations conflict. These are interpretations of the [brief](../../raw/client-brief.md), not agreed scope or targets.
