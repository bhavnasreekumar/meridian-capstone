# Where specialty grocers are opening

Saved answer, September 15, 2026. Our sources provide specific Southern California examples, but no comprehensive map of specialty-grocer openings. Locations and development status below reflect the cited articles, not a fresh verification of store operations.

## External findings

| Where | What the source says |
|---|---|
| Costa Mesa and Santa Ana, California | Gelson's opened ReCharge micromarkets paired with EV-charging hubs: 5,400 square feet in Costa Mesa and 3,000 square feet at its first location in Santa Ana. These are small convenience formats, not conventional supermarkets. [ICSC](https://www.icsc.com/news-and-views/icsc-exchange/new-grocery-formats-store-growth-and-investment-signal-sector-in-motion) |
| Redlands, California | Regency Centers is developing a shopping center anchored by a planned 36,000-square-foot Whole Foods. The article describes development, not an already-open store. [ICSC](https://www.icsc.com/news-and-views/icsc-exchange/new-grocery-formats-store-growth-and-investment-signal-sector-in-motion) |
| Southeast, especially Florida | JLL reports that the Southeast accounted for 215 of 400 tracked grocery openings in 2025. This covers grocery broadly, not specialty grocers specifically. [JLL](https://www.jll.com/en-ca/insights/market-perspectives/grocery-winners-are-built-for-value-fresh-format-and-convenience) |
| New Jersey, Maryland, and the Washington–Baltimore corridor | JLL identifies grocery expansion clusters in dense, affluent markets, describing opportunities to fill gaps and upgrade locations. It does not isolate specialty-grocer openings. [JLL](https://www.jll.com/en-ca/insights/market-perspectives/grocery-winners-are-built-for-value-fresh-format-and-convenience) |

SafeGraph supplies selection criteria rather than a documented geography of specialty openings: trade areas, competition, access, costs, and existing-store performance. [SafeGraph](https://www.safegraph.com/blog/retail-site-selection-checklist/)

These are attributed source claims. Underlying datasets were not independently verified. Review provenance and limitations in [S001: ICSC](../sources/s001.md), [S002: SafeGraph](../sources/s002.md), and [S003: JLL](../sources/s003.md).
