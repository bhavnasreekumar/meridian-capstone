# Client brief: Meridian Markets

**Source:** [Original brief](../../raw/client-brief.md), dated August 2026, from Dana Okafor, VP of Operations. This page summarizes Meridian's statements; it does not independently verify them.

## Stated situation

Meridian describes a specialty grocery business with 14 stores across Los Angeles, Orange, and Ventura counties, approximately $78 million in annual revenue, and about 620 employees. It reports growth from six to fourteen stores in five years and positioning around prepared foods, local sourcing, and smaller stores. [Original brief](../../raw/client-brief.md)

## Requested work and motivation

Dana requests a dashboard comparing sales by store and category to support the next location decision. She favors Pasadena but seeks supporting evidence. Store growth has been uneven, and leadership wants better visibility than its existing instinct-and-spreadsheet approach. Revenue, operating costs, and customer experience are stated goals without quantified targets. [Original brief](../../raw/client-brief.md)

## Data and engagement

The brief names roughly three years of POS transactions, loyalty membership and purchase history, labor scheduling and hours, and store attributes. It reports a POS migration last spring and says Marcus can arrange an extract after NDA signature. These statements do not establish that data has been received, reviewed, or approved for student access. [Original brief](../../raw/client-brief.md)

Customer and employee data must stay out of AI tools. Store-by-week sales totals and store attributes are explicitly permitted. The team's additional handling decisions are maintained in the [data-handling checklist](../../docs/data-handling-checklist.md).

Dana proposes approximately eight weeks for the project and preliminary board materials in three weeks. Confirm calendar dates rather than computing them from an unspecified briefing date. Email is her preferred channel; she reports slower responses while traveling Tuesdays and Wednesdays. [Original brief](../../raw/client-brief.md)
